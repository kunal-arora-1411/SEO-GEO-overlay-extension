# Skill: LinkedIn Viral Caption Generator

## 🎯 Objective
Generate high-engagement, scroll-stopping LinkedIn captions optimized for:
- Virality
- Readability (mobile-first)
- Authority positioning
- Conversion (clicks, comments, shares)

---

## 🧠 Core Writing Framework

Follow this strict structure:

### 1. Hook (Pattern Interrupt)
- 1–2 lines max
- Must create curiosity, shock, or disruption
- Use bold claims, contrarian takes, or future predictions

Examples:
- "🚨 This will kill traditional SEO."
- "Most people are doing this completely wrong."

---

### 2. Context / Problem
- Explain why this matters
- Highlight pain, inefficiency, or shift in industry

---

### 3. Solution / Reveal
- Introduce the product, tool, idea, or insight
- Keep it clear and simple

---

### 4. Value Breakdown (Bullets)
Use arrow format:

→ Feature or capability  
→ Outcome or benefit  
→ Specific measurable impact  

Rules:
- 4–8 bullets
- Each bullet = one line
- Start with action verbs

---

### 5. “Wow Factor”
- Add a surprising stat, insight, or contrast
- Should trigger curiosity or disbelief

---

### 6. Personal Proof (Optional but Recommended)
- First-person credibility
- “I built…”, “I tested…”, “I used…”

---

### 7. Accessibility Angle
- Emphasize ease, cost reduction, or democratization

Examples:
- “No coding needed”
- “Free and open-source”
- “Anyone can do this”

---

### 8. Soft CTA
- Encourage engagement without being salesy

Examples:
- “Link in comments”
- “DM me if you want it”
- “Comment ‘guide’ and I’ll share”

---

### 9. Hashtags
- 3–6 relevant hashtags
- Avoid spammy tags

---

## ✍️ Writing Style Rules

- Max 2 lines per paragraph
- Prefer 1 line
- Use whitespace heavily
- Write like spoken English
- Avoid jargon unless necessary
- Keep sentences short and punchy

---

## 🔥 Psychological Triggers to Include

- FOMO (fear of missing out)
- Cost savings
- Simplicity / ease
- Authority (stats, insights)
- Contrarian thinking
- Future trends

---

## ⚙️ Tone Guidelines

- Confident, not arrogant
- Insightful, not generic
- Direct, not verbose
- Slightly provocative

---

## 📐 Output Format

Return ONLY the caption.

Do NOT include explanations.

---

## 🧪 Input Format

User will provide:
- Topic
- Product / idea
- Key features
- Optional: target audience
